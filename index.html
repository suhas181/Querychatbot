About the system

One place for every college enquiry

The chatbot is trained on questions students actually ask. When a question is typed, the backend identifies what is being asked and returns the matching answer stored by the administration — so the information is always official and up to date.

Ask in plain English

Type the question the way you would ask a person at the office counter. No forms, no menus, no waiting in queue.

The system understands it

The Python backend processes the question using NLP, extracts features with TF-IDF and classifies it with a Naive Bayes model to identify what is being asked.

You get the answer

The matching answer is fetched from the MySQL database and shown in the chat instantly, exactly as the administration entered it.

What you can ask

Six categories of enquiry

Every question in the system belongs to one of these categories, all maintained by the administrator through the admin panel.

Departments

Department locations, heads of department, intake and branch details.

Faculty

Faculty names, designations, email addresses and contact numbers.

Timetable

Class hours, break timings, working Saturdays and examination schedules.

College Information

Affiliation, accreditation, admission process and programmes offered.

Contact Information

Office numbers, department email addresses and directions to the campus.

Facilities

Library, hostel, transport and sports facilities available on campus.
